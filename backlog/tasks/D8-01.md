# D8-01 — Correct the camera to an isometric read

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** The battlefield reads as a classic isometric RTS, not a near-top-down view.

**Owns:** `src/game/camera/RTSCameraController.ts` and the framing constants it reads.

**Acceptance:** the default pitch sits in the isometric band so structures show their sides and silhouettes are readable, not their roofs; buildings and Agents keep a legible footprint at every zoom level; pan bounds, zoom range, the shadow frustum, and fog still cover the visible area with no popping at the far plane; picking, box selection, and placement stay accurate under the new projection.

**Verify:** camera regression tests plus a captured frame at minimum, default, and maximum zoom.
