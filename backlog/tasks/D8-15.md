# D8-15 — Make walls actually stop what walks into them

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** A wall is worth building. Reported by a player: "what's the point of having walls if enemies can walk through them?"

**Owns:** `src/game/systems/MovementSystem.ts`, `breachTarget` in `src/game/commands/AttackCommand.ts`, the pursuit-failure branch of `src/game/systems/CombatSystem.ts`.

**Acceptance:** a unit whose route was planned before a wall existed stops at it instead of walking through it, and reroutes around it when a way round exists; a unit built around can still walk out of the cell it is standing in; an attacker with no route to its target turns on the structure blocking it rather than standing in the field; a fully sealed colony is genuinely sealed, and the army outside chews on the fence.

**Verify:** `wallsBlock.test.ts` covers the curtain raised mid-walk, the detour around a partial wall, the escape from a claimed cell, and the sealed colony being attacked; `waves.test.ts` and `soak.test.ts` keep the opponent attacking.

## Outcome

Nothing between the path planner and the renderer ever looked at the navigation grid again. A* has always refused blocked cells and never cuts a diagonal corner, so a *freshly planned* route was correct — but a route is planned once and then walked for seconds, and a wall raised across one already being walked was simply walked through. `MovementSystem` now tests every step against the grid.

Decision: only a step from open ground *into* a blocked cell is refused. Leaving a blocked cell is always allowed, because a unit can legitimately be standing in one — built around, or nudged inside a footprint's clearance — and refusing every step out would strand it for the rest of the match. An enclosure still holds: once the unit reaches open ground inside it, every step into the fence is refused.

Decision: a refused step reroutes rather than nudging or sliding along the obstacle. Three failed reroutes and the unit stops and drops the route; gather and build orders re-approach on their own, and an ordered attacker re-pursues within half a second.

Decision: an attacker that cannot reach its target attacks what is in the way. Without it, walls would have become an exploit — a sealed colony would leave an army standing in a field forever, which is a worse game than walking through the wall.

`waves.test.ts`'s cadence bound is now 420s for `relentless` and 360s for the other two. Assaults walk around structures instead of through them, which costs travel time: the relentless worst gap on seed 30 moved 335s → 381s. Measured across seeds 20-60, relaxed and standard span 170-243s and relentless spans 197-548s, so the bound still guards against decay on the seeds the test runs rather than merely recording what they happen to do.
