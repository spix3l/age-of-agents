# D8-11 — Make walls cheap enough and big enough to be worth laying

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** Fencing a colony is an affordable decision instead of an economy-wrecking one. Reported by a player: "walls cost too much to build today, they should also take more space".

**Owns:** the `wall` entry in `src/data/buildings.ts`, `buildWall` and its Generation tiers in `src/game/rendering/models/buildings.ts`, `AI.maxWalls` in `src/data/ai.ts`.

**Acceptance:** a Barrier Wall costs 10 Matter instead of 25 and covers a 4x1 footprint instead of 2x1, so a run of fence costs roughly a fifth of what it did per unit of length; the mesh matches the new footprint; segments still sit flush against each other and against a Gate in both orientations; the opponent's fence stays about the same length in world units rather than doubling.

**Verify:** `perimeter.test.ts` walks a closed rectangle at the new segment length; `PlacementController.test.ts` covers flush neighbours and the rotated stack; `waves.test.ts` holds the opponent's assault cadence.

## Outcome

Cost 25 → 10 Matter, footprint 2x1 → 4x1, HP 950 → 1100, construction 3s → 4s. Per unit of fence that is roughly a fifth of the old price for a third more health.

Decision: longer, not thicker. A 4x2 slab would have covered the same ground for the same money, but a one-deep wall is what lines up with a Gate, what a player expects to drag out, and what leaves room to build behind it.

Decision: `AI.maxWalls` 8 → 5. The constant is really "how much fence", and doubling segment length without touching it doubled the opponent's fence, which walled its own Workers in often enough to stall an assault past the cadence `waves.test.ts` enforces.
