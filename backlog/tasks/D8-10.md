# D8-10 — Let a player move a placed building

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** A colony laid out badly can be fixed without demolishing it. Reported by a player: "you can't move a building to another place".

**Owns:** `src/game/commands/RelocateCommand.ts`, the relocation branch of the placement tool in `src/game/Game.ts`, and `src/ui/actions/RelocateAction.tsx`.

**Acceptance:** selecting a completed player structure other than the Core offers MOVE BUILDING; taking it arms the existing placement ghost over that structure at its current orientation, `R` quarter-turns it, and Esc or right-click cancels; confirming a valid site re-seats the same entity — same id, HP, production queue, capacity contribution, and combat state — while releasing the old navigation cells and claiming the new ones; a structure never blocks its own destination, so a one-cell nudge is legal; a refused move leaves the structure and the grid exactly as they were; the Core and structures still under construction cannot move; relocation is free and instant, and a drag never lays a run of copies.

**Verify:** `RelocateCommand.test.ts` covers the move, the self-overlap nudge, the rotated footprint, a refused target, the excluded kinds, and a structure destroyed mid-relocation; a browser check selects a structure, moves it, and reads the directive back.

## Outcome

`issueRelocateCommand` is the single authority: it lifts the structure off the navigation grid for the check so it never collides with itself, validates with the building excluded, and either re-seats it or puts it back untouched. The placement tool is reused rather than duplicated — `Game.relocating` switches its validate and confirm callbacks — so the ghost, snapping, `R`, and cancel all behave exactly as they do when building. `BuildingEntity.footprint` and `rotated` became mutable because a relocation may quarter-turn a structure.

Decision: relocation is free and instant rather than costing a refund-and-rebuild cycle. It is a layout correction, not an economic choice, and charging for it would just push players back to demolish-and-rebuild.
