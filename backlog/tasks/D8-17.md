# D8-17 — Make the opening survivable and the command deck readable

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** Four player reports from one session: buildings could not be placed but resources looked like they had been taken; Workers wandered off and revealed the map on their own; the command deck was illegible; and "the core dies too fast, the game isn't fair enough so you would need actual building and strategy".

**Owns:** the `core` entry in `src/data/buildings.ts` and its `combat` block in `Core.ts`, `earliestAttackSeconds` across `AI_DIFFICULTY`, the attack alarm in `Game.ts` plus its store and HUD surfaces, `WorkerActions` and the command-deck CSS, `RETARGET_RANGE`.

**Acceptance:**
- Nothing is charged for a structure that was refused, and a refusal names the resource the colony is short of ("NEEDS 20 ENERGY"), not just "insufficient resources".
- A build the colony cannot pay for is dimmed and unclickable before it is armed, with the short resource marked in the cost line.
- The command deck reads as labelled groups — BUILD, KEEP GATHERING — with every unlocked structure visible at once, no clipped rows, no hint text printed over the buttons, and no overlap with the minimap.
- A Core survives a first assault long enough to be answered: 3200 HP, and it shoots back at 9 damage every 1.6s inside 11 units.
- The colony raises an alarm when a structure takes damage: a banner, a directive, a sound, and Space to put the camera on it.
- The first assault lands late enough to have built something: earliest attack 540s → 720s standard, 780s → 960s relaxed, 180s → 300s relentless.
- Fortifying is a Generation I choice: the Zap Turret unlocks at Generation I and costs 100 Matter and 45 Energy, so a colony can defend the city it is building instead of choosing between the two.
- A depleted deposit sends a Worker to the next rock in the same clearing, not across the map: the retarget radius is 22 units from its drop point, well inside the gap between one resource cluster and the next.

**Verify:** `fullLoop.integration.test.ts` asserts a refused build never charges; `ColonyActions.test.tsx` covers the dimmed state and the named shortfall; `definitionOfDone.integration.test.ts` still completes the whole flow against a defended Core; `waves.test.ts` holds the opponent's cadence; measured passive-defeat times recorded below.

## Outcome

The reported "debit without a building" was not a leak. Driving the production build through the same clicks a player makes: one Relay charged exactly 80 Matter and 20 Energy and started, and every later attempt left the ledger untouched at 35 Matter — refused because Energy was at zero, while the number the player was watching was Matter. A regression test now asserts the transaction directly. What was actually broken was the feedback around it, so the build list states affordability up front and the refusal names the shortfall.

Decision: the Core defends itself. Measured with a passive colony on the default difficulty, first damage to defeat was **29 seconds** (652s → 681s), and 1500 HP against a sixteen-Striker wave is about seven seconds of fire. At 3200 HP with a modest gun that window is roughly five minutes, and the same measurement now ends at ~890s instead of ~686s.

Decision: tuned so a Core is an objective, not a health bar. Measured against Strikers alone, eight leave it at about a fifth health and die trying; ten take it in half a minute. The opponent's standard wave of sixteen still takes it in twenty seconds, so the AI can still win — the player just has to bring an army and house it, which is what the Definition-of-Done gate now walks through.

Decision: defence has to be available while you build. Play report: "was busy building my own city then got attacked and lost" -- with the alarm working and reaching them in time. Turrets were the only answer to a raid and they sat behind Generation II, so building a city and surviving were competing plans. The Turret is now a Generation I unlock at 100 Matter and 45 Energy. Measured with a colony that builds economy and defences and never makes a single combat Agent: across three seeds on standard, unfortified it lost at 751s, 889s and 891s; fortified it lost at 755s, delayed first damage from 382s to 877s, and on the third seed survived the full forty minutes without the Core taking a scratch.

Decision: an alarm, not just a longer clock. A 300x224 map means the player is usually looking somewhere else; the banner, the sound, and Space-to-look are what turn extra Core health into a chance to answer.

Note: `waves.test.ts` and two `AIStrategy` cases moved with the pacing. The strategy cases were pinned to a literal 600s that is now inside the standard opening, and are stated relative to the preset's own constant instead. The cadence guard is now one shared helper that bounds the typical gap and allows a single outlier, which is what "does not decay" actually means.
