# D4-05 — Add health bars, command markers, and death feedback

[Back to EPIC-04](../epics/EPIC-04-war.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** Combat state and orders are readable at default zoom.

**Owns:** world-space indicators and destruction visuals.

**Acceptance:** damaged selected/nearby combatants show health; move/attack clicks create brief markers; deaths visibly disappear or collapse with a short effect; indicators dispose with entities and do not leak scene objects.

**Verify:** repeated battle scene-object count check.

## Decisions

Camera-facing health bars are attached to every unit and building visual and appear when damaged or selected. Right-click attack orders emit an orange marker; deaths emit a pooled collapse effect and dispose their visual and selectable meshes.
