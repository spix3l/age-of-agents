# D5-06 — Add AI observability and deterministic soak scenario

[Back to EPIC-05](../epics/EPIC-05-opponent.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** AI failures are diagnosable without pausing normal play.

**Owns:** `src/game/debug/`, structured logger, AI soak scenario.

**Acceptance:** F3 toggles FPS, entity count, AI state, resources, capacity, and current goal; logs use categories and can be disabled; a fixed-seed unattended match reports transitions and invariant failures without console spam.

**Verify:** run at least three deterministic soak seeds.

## Decisions

F3 toggles a debug overlay with FPS, entity counts, AI state and goal, AI economy, forces, intel, and effect pool load. `Logger` is category-filtered with a bounded buffer and an opt-in sink, so soak runs never spam the console. `runSoak` produces a deterministic report of transitions, samples, and invariant breaches; three seeds are asserted in tests.
