# D6-15 — Make colonies buildable as villages

[Back to EPIC-06](../epics/EPIC-06-evolution.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** A player can lay out an actual settlement: continuous walls in either orientation, a gate to walk through, homes, and storage.

**Owns:** Habitat/Depot/Gate config and models, per-building clearance and navigation occupancy, placement rotation and run-laying, Worker command deck.

**Acceptance:** Barrier Walls and other village pieces place edge to edge with no forced gap, while non-village structures keep their clearance; `R` quarter-turns the pending footprint and the ghost and placed model follow it; wall, gate, and habitat placement stays armed and can be dragged into a run, spending once per snapped cell; Gates take damage and block placement but never block pathing; Habitats raise Agent Capacity; Storage Depots accept deposits; all three are Awakening structures gated through `TechnologySystem`.

**Verify:** `src/game/building/village.test.ts`, `PlacementController.test.ts`, and a manual placement pass.
