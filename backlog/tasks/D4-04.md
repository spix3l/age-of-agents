# D4-04 — Add readable laser/projectile and impact effects

[Back to EPIC-04](../epics/EPIC-04-war.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** Players can see who attacked whom without coupling visuals to damage truth.

**Owns:** `src/game/rendering/EffectsManager.ts`, effect pools.

**Acceptance:** every completed attack emits a faction-readable shot and impact; effects expire and reuse pooled objects; effects do not determine hits; missing effects never block damage; effect count remains bounded in a 60-unit fight.

**Verify:** pool tests and visual battle inspection.

## Decisions

`EffectsManager` pools laser, impact, and destruction meshes behind a hard `COMBAT.maxActiveEffects` ceiling and drops overflow instead of allocating. Effects are emitted from a CombatSystem presentation hook, so damage is never gated on a visual.
