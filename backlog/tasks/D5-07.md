# D5-07 — Balance and prove the Day 5 opponent gate

[Back to EPIC-05](../epics/EPIC-05-opponent.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** An unattended AI can complete the whole match loop reliably.

**Owns:** balance values and narrow cross-system fixes; no new systems.

**Acceptance:** AI destroys an idle player's Core in at least 4 of 5 fixed-seed runs; no run deadlocks on resources, capacity, placement, production, or pathing; median idle-player loss is within 8–20 minutes; P0 checks remain green.

**Verify:** record seeds, durations, outcomes, and failures in `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.

## Decisions

Balance lives entirely in `src/data/ai.ts`. Five fixed seeds (10/20/30/40/50) all end in an AI victory against an idle player with a median of 8.5 minutes and zero invariant failures. Balancing surfaced a real deadlock — a fully mined map could never reach the attack threshold — fixed in the strategy with a sustained-reinforcement-stall fallback rather than by hiding it behind numbers.
