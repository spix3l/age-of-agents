# D8-09 — Rebuild the visual language as clean sci-fi

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** The game looks futuristic and deliberate instead of dark and flat.

**Reference:** `models/tile_*.png` — the project owner's art direction. Bright
white armour plates over a dark navy chassis, glossy cyan emissive strips, amber
secondary accents, tiered octagonal bases, corner buttress pods, antenna spires,
dish arrays, chunky mech and tracked-vehicle silhouettes.

**Owns:** `src/game/rendering/`, `src/game/world/environment.ts`, `src/styles/global.css`.

**Acceptance:** the material language matches the reference — light panels read
white under key light, emissives glow, surfaces are glossy rather than matte;
every building and Agent kind is rebuilt on a shared kit of tiers, plates, pods,
strips, and spires so the set reads as one faction's engineering; lighting,
tone mapping, and exposure are tuned for that contrast; terrain no longer
competes with the structures; the HUD and combat effects share the same palette;
faction identity, Generation upgrades, selection, damage, and fog all still read;
everything stays procedural with no asset-loading dependency; a 100-unit battle
holds its frame budget.

**Verify:** `?scenario=showcase` captures at default and minimum zoom, model
tests, and a frame-rate re-measurement.

## Progress

**Done.** The material language is rebuilt against `models/` and the owner's
north-star mockup: gunmetal armour and layered plates over a dark navy chassis,
cyan emissive seams, amber hazard trim, smooth shading, glossier and more
metallic surfaces. Lighting is a single hard key plus a cool rim, with exposure
and bloom tuned so only the seams glow. The `Kit` gained `podRing`, `chassis`,
and `seam` — the vocabulary the reference builds from. Terrain carries three
scales of noise with bare earth through thin grass and scree on slopes.

**Two course corrections, recorded so they are not repeated.** The camera must
not be yawed; and the world stays green — an earlier dark-slate ground made the
valley compete with the structures and was wrong.

**Left to do.** Individual structures are still simpler than the reference:
they need the layered tiers, corner buttress pods, dishes and spires the new
`Kit` helpers now make available, applied per building kind. The HUD has the
minimap and income readouts but not the north star's selection portrait panel,
tabbed build palette with cost chips, or top-bar controls. Combat effects have
not been restyled.
