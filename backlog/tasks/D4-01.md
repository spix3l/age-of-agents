# D4-01 — Add teams, health, damage, and destruction lifecycle

[Back to EPIC-04](../epics/EPIC-04-war.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** Units and buildings share reliable hostility, damage, and cleanup rules.

**Owns:** combat components, `DamageService`, destruction queue.

**Acceptance:** friendly fire is rejected; HP clamps to zero/max; death is processed once after system iteration; destroyed entities lose selection/orders/capacity/occupancy; stats attribute kills and losses.

**Verify:** damage and cleanup tests for unit and building victims.

## Decisions

Combat stats live in `src/data/units.ts` and `src/data/combat.ts`. `DamageService` is the single HP writer and queues each death once; `src/game/combat/destruction.ts` performs the shared cleanup (selection, orders, combat targets, Agent Capacity, navigation occupancy, queued reservations) for both Game and headless tests. `MatchStats` attributes kills and losses to both teams.
