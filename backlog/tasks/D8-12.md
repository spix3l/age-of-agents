# D8-12 — Open the map out and stop drawing a fence around it

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** The ground you can see is ground you can use, and where it ends it ends for a visible reason. Reported by a player, with a screenshot of the boundary: "remove the delimiter around the playable map, the whole big map should be navigable".

**Owns:** `MAP_BOUNDS` and `MAP_MARGIN` in `src/game/world/map.ts`, the terrain ramp, edge shading, tree line, and ground cover in `src/game/world/environment.ts`, the fog overlay in `src/game/world/WorldScene.ts`.

**Acceptance:** the playable rectangle grows from 240x176 to 300x224 with the start positions unchanged, so openings and walking distances between the colonies are untouched; the perimeter tree line is gone; the land past the bounds climbs into hillside within about twenty units instead of easing up over forty; the fog overlay reaches out over the scenery margin so the darkness beyond the bounds is continuous with the darkness inside it; the camera, minimap, navigation grid, and vision grid all follow the new bounds.

**Verify:** `mapGeneration.test.ts` and `createMatch.test.ts` keep generated deposits inside the bounds and reachable; `pathing.regression.test.ts` still routes corner to corner; a browser check pans to the west edge and reads a hillside rather than a line.

## Outcome

The reported line was three things at once: a tree line planted a stride inside the bounds, a hard shading band at the boundary, and a fog overlay that stopped exactly there while the lit meadow carried on past it. All three are gone or extended, and the border is now a wooded hillside.

Decision: the bounds grew but `START_POSITIONS` did not move. Every balance figure, opening, and assault timing is tuned against the distance between the two corners; the extra ground is open country added around the outside, so nothing tuned had to be re-tuned.

Decision: the whole scenery margin was not made playable. Beyond the new bounds the terrain is hills, water, and distant ranges that the simulation has no obstacle data for; opening it would mean flattening the horizon. Raising the ramp so the edge reads as a hillside answers the complaint the flat apron created.
