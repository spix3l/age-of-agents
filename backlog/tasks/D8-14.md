# D8-14 — Add Freestyle mode

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** A way to play the game for the colony rather than the fight. Requested by a player: "a freestyle mode where you don't have to rush against AI, you just build a village and evolve the technology".

**Owns:** the `solo` option through `createMatch` and `createEconomyScenario`, `mode` in `src/ui/store.ts` and `src/game/Game.ts`, the mode picker in `src/ui/menus/MainMenu.tsx`.

**Acceptance:** the main menu offers CAMPAIGN and FREESTYLE, and the opponent picker only shows for Campaign; Freestyle generates the same seeded map with the same mirrored deposits and no opposing colony, and runs no opponent at all; nothing can end a Freestyle match, so the end screen never appears; the HUD directive says what the mode is for; a Freestyle match saves and resumes as Freestyle.

**Verify:** `freestyle.test.ts` proves the solo scenario keeps every deposit and drops every enemy entity, and that ten minutes of simulation leaves the match unfinished with no opponent; a browser check plays Freestyle, saves, and resumes it.

## Outcome

Freestyle is the shipping world with nobody in the far corner. The generator drops the opponent's Core and Workers and keeps its half of the deposits, so the whole map is still worth crossing, and `MatchSimulation` is constructed with `opponent: false`.

Decision: the map is not re-tuned for solo play. The mirrored layout is what makes the far half worth walking to, and a player who wants the whole map should get the whole map.
