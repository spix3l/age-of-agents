## Decisions

`MilitaryAI` keeps each Fabricator's queue shallow so shortages fall back to the economy and build slices, holds finished Strikers at a Core-side assembly point, and keeps `AI.defenseReserve` units home when an assault launches. Army orders are re-issued on an `AI.ordersInterval` cooldown so pursuit never repaths every decision tick.
