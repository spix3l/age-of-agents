# D5-03 — Implement AI building and capacity management

[Back to EPIC-05](../epics/EPIC-05-opponent.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** The AI builds valid Relay Nodes and a Fabricator before capacity or production stalls.

**Owns:** `src/game/ai/BuildPlanner.ts` and placement scoring.

**Acceptance:** build sites are reachable, valid, and near but not blocking the Core; AI reserves affordability sensibly; capacity is built before saturation; lost essential buildings are rebuilt; repeated placement failure backs off and chooses another site.

**Verify:** normal and blocker-heavy base scenarios.

## Decisions

`BuildPlanner` scores random ring candidates around the Core through the same `validatePlacement` a player uses, prefers close-but-uncrowded sites, re-assigns builders to stalled sites, rebuilds lost essentials, and backs a building type off for `AI.placementBackoffSeconds` after repeated placement failures. A walled-in base was verified to back off instead of spinning.
