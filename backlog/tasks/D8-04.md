# D8-04 — Add a minimap

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** The player can see the whole battlefield and move around it at a glance.

**Owns:** `src/ui/hud/Minimap.tsx` and its snapshot in `src/ui/store.ts`.

**Acceptance:** the minimap shows terrain, both factions' known entities, and resources, obeying Fog of War exactly as the world does — unseen hostiles never appear; the camera viewport is drawn and clicking or dragging moves the camera; it updates on the throttled snapshot, never per frame, and adds no measurable cost to a 100-unit battle; it works at both supported viewport sizes.

**Verify:** fog-agreement and camera-jump tests, plus a frame-rate re-measurement.
