# D5-05 — Implement AI scouting, defense, attack, and recovery

[Back to EPIC-05](../epics/EPIC-05-opponent.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** The AI discovers the player, responds to attacks, launches grouped assaults, and rebuilds after losses.

**Owns:** AI knowledge memory and military behavior slices.

**Acceptance:** player's Core must be observed before becoming an attack target; nearby threats trigger DEFEND; attack requires a tunable force threshold and sends a group; heavy losses trigger RECOVER; stale/dead targets clear safely; AI can resume attacking after recovery.

**Verify:** scripted discovery, base raid, failed assault, and recovery scenarios.

## Decisions

The AI must observe the player Core with one of its own entities before it can target it; `AIKnowledge` records sightings and forgets destroyed entities so stale targets are never pursued. Scouts sweep the mirrored start position and map corners. Base threats force DEFEND, an assault needs `AI.attackForce` (or a sustained reinforcement stall), losing `AI.recoverLossRatio` of the army triggers RECOVER, and the AI resumes attacking afterwards.
