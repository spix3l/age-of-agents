# D8-08 — Give the opponent a real colony and real defenses

[Back to EPIC-08](../epics/EPIC-08-depth.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** Attacking the opponent's base feels like attacking a base, not a Core with two sheds.

**Owns:** `src/game/ai/BuildPlanner.ts`, `src/data/ai.ts`.

**Acceptance:** the opponent builds the full catalog it has unlocked — Relays, Fabricators, Habitats, Storage Depots, and at Generation II Turrets — instead of the current handful; it fortifies, placing Turrets covering its Core and its approach rather than never defending; structures are laid out as a colony, not scattered on a ring; it keeps building while an assault is out, and rebuilds what it loses; the build plan scales with difficulty and never spends the Matter its army needs.

**Verify:** a test asserting building count, kind variety, and Turret coverage by a fixed point in the match, plus re-measured soaks.
