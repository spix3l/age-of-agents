# D1-08 — Implement click and drag-box selection

[Back to EPIC-01](../epics/EPIC-01-world.md) · [Backlog index](../../BACKLOG.md)

**Outcome:** The player can single-select and box-select friendly units and issue contextual terrain moves.

**Owns:** `src/game/input/`, `src/game/systems/SelectionSystem.ts`, `src/ui/selection/`.

**Acceptance:** click raycasting, drag threshold, box selection, empty-click deselection, and Shift additive selection work; enemy entities cannot join the controllable selection; selected units have visible rings; right-click terrain submits `MoveCommand` with a marker.

**Verify:** manual selection matrix and selection math tests.
