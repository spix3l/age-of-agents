## Root causes found this pass (all verified live in the Model Lab)

1. **Dead referencePbr maps.** `attach_reference_pbr` wired the extracted channel maps as
   absolute filesystem paths (`/Users/...`), which `THREE.TextureLoader` can never fetch from
   the dev server: every albedo/roughness edit to the spec was silently ignored and the steel
   rendered an empty black map. The raw extractions are also pixel patches from the 156 px cell
   (background checker, intruding amber bar) and render as garbage when tiled. Fix:
   `scripts/make-pbr-canvases.py` synthesizes clean 256 px tiling maps from the authored
   palette, served from `src/game/rendering/models/generated/evidence/core/`; raw extractions
   stay as review evidence under `extractedFrom` with provenance in the spec.
2. **Smooth-shaded lathes melted.** Lathe profile steps interpolated normals across kinks and
   smeared broad speculars over the drum skirt and plinth top. `flatShading: true` on the
   hard-surface materials restored the reference's crisp per-face value steps.
3. **Shared environment probe washed or starved.** scene.environmentIntensity 0.4 turned dark
   metal to mid grey; 0.12 starved it. The lab now runs 0.15 and each material carries its own
   `envMapIntensity` (steel 0.5, white armour 0.7, emissives 0.0).
4. **Emissive intensity clipped to white under ACES.** Cyan 3.2 → 1.6, amber 2.4 → 1.4,
   beam 4.0 → 1.5 holds the hues.

## Two-tone re-author (current, proportions pass 2)

- New `armour-white` material is the dominant read: pods, caps, drum faces, crown buttresses,
  portal pylon. `steel-dark` is now the structural dark (plinth, seat rings, louvres, gate).
- Octagonal tiers (plinth, drum, and all rings 6 → 8); amber fascia per face; corner antenna
  masts with cyan tips (4 × 2 components); pod cyan slot + horizontal louvre bank; crown is 8
  gently-leaning white buttresses around a **glowing cyan emitter dome** (new `core-dome`
  sphere, half-sunk in the basin) with the beam rising out of it; beam core 0.17 inside a
  0.36 halo shell.
- Proportions pass 2: tiers squatted (RING 0.40 / DRUM 0.42 / COLLAR 0.34, body 1.38), plinth
  widened to 2.6, ring body 1.9, drum 1.52, pods 0.58-wide flush-capped panels at radius 0.98,
  antennas at radius 1.2. Matches the reference's squat wide-silhouette read.

## Still not matching the reference

- No bloom pass in the lab: the pool and beam glow read as bright paint, and the beam ends
  abruptly where the reference fades. (The generated factory ships an unused
  `createCoreKeepPresentationComposer` with UnrealBloom; wiring it into the lab viewer is the
  single remaining fidelity lever.)
- The rear hemisphere is generated by radial repetition, not observed.
- `diagnose_render` silhouette IoU still fails on framing: the tool auto-frames the model
  including the beam (which doubles the height), while the reference crop is body-dominated.
  The failure is a framing artifact, not a form mismatch — the turntable gate passes clean.

## Stated approximations

- Radial order 8 now read from the high-res reference; the earlier 6-fold stylization is retired.
- Bolt heads, the status panel and seam widths remain plausible, not measured.
- The beam is volumeless light; additive emissive geometry is a representation choice.
